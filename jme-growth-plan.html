Part 1 · Fix first

What is costing you bookings today

Each of these is on the live site as of this review. They are small and quick to fix, but patients see them.

Urgent

The Liosban call button on the Fees page rings Devon Park

The button reads "Call Liosban 091 342 555" but dials 091 520 222. A Tuam Road patient who taps it reaches the wrong clinic.

Fix: change the link to tel:091342555. Two minutes in Squarespace.

Urgent

A setup message appears in the newsletter block on every page

The page content includes "This newsletter signup form needs a storage option…". To a new patient it looks like an unfinished site.

Fix: connect the form to a mailing list or remove the block.

High

Prices are only in images

The Fees page shows the price list as around 13 images. Google cannot read them, so searches like "dental cleaning price Galway" or "root canal cost Galway" never find you, and phone users have to zoom in.

Fix: put the main prices in text (see Part 3).

High

Opening hours differ between the site and Google Maps

Google Maps shows Liosban opening at 9am, while the website says 8.30am. Devon Park's late Thursday is 8.30pm on the About page and 8pm on the homepage, Contact and Fees pages. When hours don't match, Google trusts the listing less, and early patients may find the door closed or not call.

Fix: agree one set of hours per clinic and use it on every page and on both Google Business Profiles.

Tidy up

Small copy mismatches

The cancellation policy says "Devon Park Dental". One review mentions "Dr Ali Browne" while the team page lists Dr Ali Alzein. Dr Ellen Burke is on Meet the Team but not on About, and Olwyn Duffy is on About but not on Meet the Team. Recent Google reviews praise a Dr Kevin at Devon Park, who isn't on the team page yet.

Fix: one pass over the copy.

Part 3 · Convert

Turn more visitors into booked appointments

3.1

Prices in text, with savings shown first

Many people are worried about cost before they book. Your strongest selling points are hidden at the bottom of the Fees page. Moving them to the top gives a reason to book now:

  • Free annual check-up for eligible PRSI workers, with a subsidised scale and polish
  • 20% tax relief on eligible treatments through Revenue
  • Insurance accepted: VHI, Laya, Irish Life and others

Below that, a simple text table with "from" prices for the 10 most searched treatments (check-up, hygiene, fillings, extraction, root canal, crowns, implants, Invisalign, whitening, anti-wrinkle). The image price list can stay for the full detail.

3.2

More Google reviews, every week

Reviews are one of the biggest factors in local ranking, and the first thing new patients compare. Your patients already rate you highly: 4.6★ from 193 reviews at Devon Park and 4.8★ from 53 at Liosban. Two things hold that back:

  • Liosban has under a third of Devon Park's reviews, so it carries less weight in Tuam Road and north-side searches.
  • The website doesn't show these ratings. It has about 11 text testimonials with no stars or source.

A simple routine fixes both: a QR code card at each reception desk, one text message sent the day after a visit, and the live Google rating shown on each clinic page. A realistic goal is Liosban passing 100 reviews within six months.

Text message after a visit
Hi [Name], thanks for visiting JME Dental [Devon Park / Liosban] yesterday. If you were happy with your visit, a quick Google review helps other Galway families find us: [review link]. Thank you! – The JME team

Each clinic has its own review link, so reviews go to the right location in Google Maps. Once there are reviews, they can be shown on the clinic pages with stars.

3.3

A short FAQ on the questions patients already ask

A FAQ answers concerns before the patient picks up the phone. It also helps you appear in Google's question boxes and AI answers. Your Google reviews show what matters most to patients: feeling at ease when nervous, getting seen quickly in pain, how children are treated, and cost. Draft questions:

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Many of our patients feel anxious before a visit. Tell us when you book and we'll take things at your pace, explain each step and make sure you're comfortable.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes, at both Galway clinics. Book online or call Devon Park on 091 520 222 or Liosban on 091 342 555.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. We keep emergency appointments, and our 24-hour line is 086 306 2400.

Is my check-up free with PRSI?

If you are eligible for the Treatment Benefit Scheme, your annual check-up is free and your cleaning is subsidised. We can check eligibility for you.

Can I claim tax relief on dental treatment?

You can claim 20% tax relief on eligible non-routine treatments, such as crowns, root canal treatment and implants, through Revenue.

Which clinic is closer to me?

Devon Park is on Salthill Road Lower, near Salthill. Liosban is on the Tuam Road, in the Liosban Industrial Estate on the north side of the city.

Do you have late or Saturday appointments?

Devon Park is open late on Thursdays and on Saturday mornings. Liosban offers late appointments on request.
